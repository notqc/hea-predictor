Disclaimer. Predictions are computational estimates from a Support Vector Regression model trained on 447 literature hardness records (MPEA dataset, Borg et al., Sci. Data 7, 430, 2020). They are not experimentally validated measurements. Hardness depends strongly on processing (casting, annealing, powder metallurgy), which this composition-based model does not take as input; typical error is about 100 HV for alloys similar to the training data and about 140 HV for data from a new laboratory. CALPHAD phase fractions are not calculated here. The uncertainty shown is the predictive standard deviation of a companion Gaussian-process model, not a confidence percentage. The indent drawing is an illustration: the diagonal a Vickers indent would have at a 10 kgf load for the predicted HV (HV = 1.8544 F/d²).

This page runs the same exported model and feature equations as the project's Streamlit app (08_Web_Application); predictions agree to within 0.0001 HV.
